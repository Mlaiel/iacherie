## Overview

The Legal Compliance Database Module provides comprehensive legal compliance management for the IA Influencer Agent + Content Protection Platform. This enterprise-grade module handles copyright management, GDPR compliance, DMCA takedown processes, licensing automation, and regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions for multi-format content creators (musicians, bloggers, photographers, influencers, comedians).
