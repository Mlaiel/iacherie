## 🌟 General Platform Questions

### What is Ainflue?
**A:** Ainflue is an AI-powered content protection and monetization platform that helps creators protect their digital content from unauthorized use while maximizing revenue across multiple platforms. We use advanced AI fingerprinting technology to detect content violations and provide automated DMCA takedown services.

### Who can use Ainflue?
**A:** Ainflue is designed for:
- Content creators (musicians, video producers, artists, writers)
- Digital agencies managing multiple creators
- Enterprises protecting proprietary content
- Independent artists and influencers
- Media companies and publishers

### What makes Ainflue different from other protection platforms?
**A:** Ainflue combines:
- **AI-Powered Detection:** Advanced machine learning for accurate content matching
- **Multi-Platform Monitoring:** 50+ platforms including social media, streaming, and content sites
- **Revenue Optimization:** Integrated monetization tracking and optimization
- **Collaboration Tools:** AI-powered creator matching and project management
- **Global Support:** 350+ languages and cultural localization

### How accurate is the AI content detection?
**A:** Our AI detection system achieves:
- **Audio Content:** 98.5% accuracy with < 0.1% false positives
- **Video Content:** 96.8% accuracy with < 0.3% false positives
- **Image Content:** 99.2% accuracy with < 0.05% false positives
- **Text Content:** 94.7% accuracy with < 0.5% false positives

### What platforms does Ainflue monitor?
**A:** We monitor 50+ platforms including:
- **Social Media:** YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn
- **Music Platforms:** Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp
- **Video Platforms:** Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch

- **And many more...**

## 🛡️ Protection & Monitoring

### How does content protection work?
**A:** Our protection system works in three steps:
1. **Fingerprinting:** AI creates unique digital signatures
2. **Monitoring:** Continuous scanning across platforms
3. **Action:** Automated detection and takedown processes

### How often does Ainflue scan for violations?
**A:** Scanning frequency depends on your plan:
- **Free:** Weekly scans
- **Premium:** Daily scans
- **Enterprise:** Real-time monitoring (every 4 hours)

### What happens when a violation is detected?
**A:** When violations are found:
1. **Immediate Alert:** Notification sent via email/SMS
2. **Evidence Collection:** Screenshots and metadata captured
3. **Confidence Scoring:** AI rates match accuracy (0-100%)
4. **Action Options:** Manual review or automated takedown
5. **Progress Tracking:** Monitor resolution status

### How effective are DMCA takedowns?
**A:** Our DMCA success rates:
- **YouTube:** 95% success within 48 hours
- **Instagram:** 90% success within 72 hours
- **TikTok:** 85% success within 5 days
- **Other Platforms:** 80% average success rate

### Can I whitelist certain uses of my content?
**A:** Yes! You can create whitelists for:
- **Authorized Users:** People allowed to use your content
- **Platforms:** Sites where you've given permission
- **Content Types:** Specific use cases (reviews, tutorials)
- **Time Periods:** Temporary permissions

### What if I get a false positive?
**A:** False positives can be easily managed:
1. **Review Alert:** Check the detected violation
2. **Mark as False Positive:** Click the "Not a violation" button
3. **Automatic Learning:** AI learns from your feedback
4. **Whitelist Option:** Add to whitelist to prevent future alerts

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### My files won't upload. What should I do?
**A:** Common upload solutions:
1. **Check File Size:** Ensure within plan limits
2. **File Format:** Verify supported format
3. **Internet Connection:** Test connection stability
4. **Browser Cache:** Clear cache and cookies
5. **Disable Ad Blockers:** Temporarily disable extensions
6. **Try Different Browser:** Use alternative browser

### I'm not receiving violation alerts. Why?
**A:** Alert troubleshooting:
1. **Check Spam Folder:** Alerts might be filtered
2. **Notification Settings:** Verify alert preferences
3. **Email Verification:** Confirm email address is verified
4. **Monitoring Status:** Ensure monitoring is active
5. **Plan Limits:** Check if scan limits exceeded

### The platform is running slowly. How can I fix this?
**A:** Performance optimization:
1. **Browser Update:** Use latest browser version
2. **Clear Cache:** Remove temporary files
3. **Close Tabs:** Reduce browser memory usage
4. **Check Extensions:** Disable unnecessary extensions
5. **Internet Speed:** Test connection speed

### I can't log into my account. What should I do?
**A:** Login troubleshooting:
1. **Password Reset:** Use "Forgot Password" link
2. **Clear Cookies:** Remove stored login data
3. **Check Caps Lock:** Verify password case
4. **MFA Issues:** Use backup codes if available
5. **Account Status:** Contact support if account suspended

### My revenue data looks incorrect. How do I fix it?
**A:** Revenue troubleshooting:
1. **Platform Connections:** Verify all platforms connected
2. **Permission Settings:** Check platform API permissions
3. **Currency Settings:** Confirm correct currency selected
4. **Date Ranges:** Verify selected time periods
5. **Sync Status:** Force manual data sync
