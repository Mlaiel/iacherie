## 💰 Overview

Revenue Tracking helps content creators monitor earnings across all platforms, analyze the financial impact of content protection, and optimize monetization strategies. Our system integrates with 50+ platform APIs to provide real-time revenue insights.

## 📊 Key Features

### Multi-Platform Revenue Tracking
- **Platform Support**: 50+ major platforms with API integration
- **Real-Time Sync**: Automatic revenue data synchronization
- **Currency Support**: 150+ currencies with real-time conversion
- **Historical Data**: Up to 5 years of revenue history

### Protection ROI Analysis
- **Impact Measurement**: Quantify revenue loss from violations
- **Recovery Tracking**: Monitor revenue recovered through protection
- **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: Protection costs vs. revenue saved
- **Trend Analysis**: Long-term protection effectiveness

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Data Synchronization Problems
**Symptoms**: Missing revenue data, outdated information
**Solutions**:
- Check platform API connections
- Verify data permissions
- Force manual synchronization
- Contact platform support if needed

#### Currency Conversion Issues
**Symptoms**: Incorrect amounts, exchange rate problems
**Solutions**:
- Verify base currency settings
- Check exchange rate sources
- Review conversion timestamps
- Update currency preferences

#### Payment Tracking Errors
**Symptoms**: Missing payments, incorrect amounts
**Solutions**:
- Verify payment method connections
- Check platform payment schedules
- Review currency and fee deductions
- Cross-reference with platform statements
