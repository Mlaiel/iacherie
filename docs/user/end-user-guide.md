# 📚 Ainflue Platform - Complete End-User Guide

## 🎯 Welcome to Ainflue

Ainflue is an AI-powered content protection and monetization platform designed for creators, brands, and agencies. This comprehensive guide will help you master all platform features with step-by-step instructions and visual examples.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

#### Step 1: Registration Process
1. Navigate to [https://app.ainflue.com/register](https://app.ainflue.com/register)
2. Choose your account type:
   - **Creator**: Individual content creators, artists, musicians
   - **Brand**: Companies looking to protect and monetize content
   - **Agency**: Marketing agencies managing multiple accounts

![Registration Screen](screenshots/registration-screen.png)
*Screenshot: Account type selection during registration*

3. Fill in your details:
   - Email address (will be your login username)
   - Strong password (minimum 8 characters, including numbers and symbols)
   - Username (publicly visible, choose carefully)
   - Country/Region for compliance

![Registration Form](screenshots/registration-form.png)
*Screenshot: Complete registration form*

4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. Complete your profile setup

#### Step 2: Profile Setup
Your profile is the foundation of your Ainflue experience:

1. **Profile Picture**: Upload a high-quality image (recommended: 400x400px, PNG/JPG)
2. **Bio**: Describe yourself or your brand (max 500 characters)
3. **Categories**: Select your content categories (music, video, art, etc.)
4. **Links**: Add your social media and website links
5. **Verification**: Submit documents for account verification (recommended)

![Profile Setup](screenshots/profile-setup.png)
*Screenshot: Profile setup interface*

**Tips for Better Profile Setup:**
- Use a professional headshot or logo
- Write a compelling bio that explains your unique value

- Verify your account to unlock premium features

## 📤 Content Upload & Management

### Uploading Your First Content

#### Supported File Types
- **Audio**: MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC (max 100MB)
- **Video**: MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV (max 500MB)
- **Images**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP (max 50MB)
- **Documents**: PDF (max 25MB)

#### Upload Process

1. Click the **"Upload Content"** button on your dashboard
2. Select files by dragging and dropping or clicking "Browse Files"

![Upload Interface](screenshots/upload-interface.png)
*Screenshot: File upload interface*

3. For each file, provide:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive name
   - **Description**: Detailed description (helps with discovery)

*Screenshot: Content details form*

## 🛡️ AI-Powered Content Protection

### Understanding Protection Features

Ainflue's AI automatically protects your content through:
- **Fingerprinting**: Creates unique digital signatures
- **Monitoring**: Scans platforms for unauthorized use
- **Detection**: Identifies copies and derivatives
- **Enforcement**: Automated takedown requests

### Setting Up Protection

#### Basic Protection Setup
1. Navigate to **"Protection"** → **"Settings"**
2. Enable protection for content types:
   - Audio fingerprinting
   - Video analysis
   - Image recognition
   - Text similarity detection

![Protection Settings](screenshots/protection-settings.png)
*Screenshot: Protection configuration panel*

#### Advanced Protection Options
- **Sensitivity Level**: How strict the detection should be
- **Platforms to Monitor**: Choose which platforms to scan
- **Action Preferences**: Auto-takedown vs. notification only
- **Whitelist**: Approved users who can use your content

#### Monitoring Platforms
Ainflue monitors content across:
- YouTube
- Instagram
- TikTok
- Facebook
- Twitter
- SoundCloud
- Spotify
- Custom URL monitoring

![Platform Monitoring](screenshots/platform-monitoring.png)

### Managing Protection Alerts

#### Alert Types
- **Direct Copy**: Exact duplicate of your content
- **Derivative Work**: Modified version of your content
- **Partial Use**: Segments of your content used
- **Suspicious Activity**: Potential unauthorized use

#### Reviewing Alerts
1. Go to **"Protection"** → **"Alerts"**
2. Review each detection:
   - Compare original vs. detected content
   - View platform and user information
   - Check similarity percentage
   - Review automated analysis

![Protection Alerts](screenshots/protection-alerts.png)
*Screenshot: Protection alerts dashboard*

#### Taking Action on Alerts
For each alert, you can:
- **Approve Takedown**: Send DMCA takedown request
- **Contact User**: Send message to content user
- **Whitelist**: Allow this use permanently
- **Dismiss**: Mark as false positive
- **Escalate**: Request manual review

## 🆘 Troubleshooting & Support

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### Upload Problems
**Issue**: Files won't upload
**Solutions**:
1. Check file size limits (audio: 100MB, video: 500MB)
2. Verify file format is supported
3. Clear browser cache and cookies
4. Try a different browser
5. Check internet connection stability

**Issue**: Upload fails at 99%
**Solutions**:
1. Don't close browser during upload
2. Disable browser extensions temporarily
3. Try uploading smaller batches
4. Use wired internet connection

#### Protection Issues
**Issue**: No protection alerts received
**Solutions**:
1. Verify protection is enabled in settings
2. Check that platforms are selected for monitoring
3. Ensure content has been processed (24-48 hours)
4. Verify contact email is correct

**Issue**: False positive alerts
**Solutions**:
1. Adjust sensitivity settings
2. Whitelist authorized users
3. Review and dismiss false alerts
4. Contact support for algorithm improvements

#### Payment Issues
**Issue**: Payment not received
**Solutions**:
1. Check payment method details are correct
2. Verify you've reached minimum payout threshold
3. Check for pending verification requirements
4. Review transaction history for holds

**Issue**: Incorrect earnings calculation
**Solutions**:
1. Review revenue sharing agreements
2. Check for processing fees
3. Verify tax withholdings
4. Contact support with specific transaction details
