## Getting Started

### Q: What is Ainflue and how does it work?
**A:** Ainflue is an AI-powered content protection and monetization platform. It creates unique digital fingerprints of your content, monitors 200+ platforms for unauthorized use, and automatically sends DMCA takedown notices when violations are detected.

### Q: How long does it take to see results?
**A:** Content fingerprinting takes 5-30 minutes. Violation monitoring is continuous, but new content may take 24-48 hours to start showing in search results across platforms. First violations are typically detected within 1-2 weeks for popular content.

### Q: What types of content can I protect?
**A:** We support video files (MP4, AVI, MOV, etc.), audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, etc.), and images (JPG, PNG, etc.). Maximum file size is 10GB for Free accounts, 100GB for Premium+.

### Q: Do I need technical knowledge to use Ainflue?
**A:** No! Our platform is designed for creators without technical backgrounds. The interface is intuitive, and most features work automatically after initial setup.

## Account & Billing

### Q: What's included in the Free plan?
**A:** Free plan includes 100MB storage, basic protection, 10 scans/month, email support, and access to community features. Perfect for testing the platform.

### Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
**A:** Yes, you can change plans instantly. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle to ensure uninterrupted service.

### Q: How does billing work?
**A:** All plans are billed monthly. We accept credit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency. Enterprise plans can be billed annually with discounts available.

### Q: Is there a refund policy?
**A:** Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for paid plans. No questions asked. Free plan users can upgrade with confidence.

### Q: What happens if I cancel my subscription?
**A:** Your account remains active until the end of the billing period. After that, you're moved to the Free plan. Your data is preserved for 90 days for easy reactivation.

## Content Upload & Protection

### Q: How accurate is the AI fingerprinting?
**A:** Our AI achieves 99.2% accuracy in content recognition. It can detect modified versions, including cropped, filtered, or slightly altered content.

### Q: Can I protect copyrighted content I don't own?
**A:** No. You must own the rights to any content you upload. Uploading copyrighted material you don't own violates our terms of service and could result in account suspension.

### Q: What if my content gets falsely claimed?
**A:** Our AI continuously learns and improves. False positives are rare (<0.8%) but if they occur, you can dispute claims through our platform. We also provide evidence for all detections.

### Q: How long does fingerprinting take?
**A:** Processing time depends on file size and complexity:
- Images: 30 seconds - 2 minutes
- Audio: 2-10 minutes  
- Video: 5-30 minutes
- Large files (>1GB): Up to 2 hours

### Q: Can I protect content that's already online?
**A:** Yes! You can protect existing content by uploading it to our platform. We'll start monitoring immediately and can detect unauthorized copies that already exist online.

## Monitoring & Detection

### Q: How many platforms do you monitor?
**A:** We monitor 200+ platforms including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter, SoundCloud, Spotify, and many others. Our coverage is constantly expanding.

### Q: How often do you scan for violations?
**A:** Major platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) are scanned in real-time. Other platforms are scanned daily to weekly depending on their size and API limitations.

### Q: What counts as a violation?
**A:** A violation is detected when your protected content appears on a platform without authorization. Our AI can detect exact matches and similar content (modified, cropped, filtered versions).

### Q: Do you detect content across different languages/regions?
**A:** Yes! Our monitoring is global and language-agnostic. We detect content regardless of titles, descriptions, or metadata language.

### Q: Can I whitelist certain accounts or platforms?
**A:** Yes, you can create whitelists for authorized distributors, collaborators, or platforms where you've given permission for your content to appear.

## DMCA & Legal

### Q: Are your DMCA notices legally valid?
**A:** Yes, our DMCA notices are legally compliant and include all required elements. They're prepared by legal experts and automatically customized for each platform's requirements.

### Q: What's your success rate with takedowns?
**A:** Overall success rate is 89%. Platform-specific rates: YouTube (95%), Instagram (90%), TikTok (85%), Facebook (92%). Success rates vary by platform policies and response times.

### Q: How long do takedowns take?
**A:** Response times vary by platform:
- YouTube: 24-48 hours (95% success)
- Instagram: 48-72 hours (90% success)
- TikTok: 3-7 days (85% success)
- Others: 1-14 days depending on platform

### Q: Can I send manual DMCA notices?
**A:** Yes, Premium+ users can send custom DMCA notices and track their status. We provide templates and guidance for effective takedown requests.

### Q: What if a platform rejects my DMCA notice?
**A:** We provide detailed rejection reasons and help you submit improved notices. For persistent cases, we can connect you with legal partners for escalation.

## Revenue Tracking

### Q: How do you track my revenue?
**A:** We connect to platform APIs (YouTube Analytics, Facebook Creator Studio, etc.) to track your earnings. All data is encrypted and securely stored.

### Q: Can you track revenue from all platforms?
**A:** We support revenue tracking for 50+ major platforms. Some smaller platforms may require manual data entry or API setup assistance.

### Q: How do you calculate protection ROI?
**A:** We analyze revenue trends before and after protection activation, identify revenue loss from violations, and calculate recovered earnings from successful takedowns.

### Q: Is my financial data safe?
**A:** Yes, we use bank-level encryption (AES-256) and never store actual payment information. We only access anonymized revenue metrics through secure APIs.

### Q: Can I export my revenue data?
**A:** Yes, you can export reports in PDF, Excel, or CSV formats. Reports include detailed breakdowns by platform, content, and time period.

## Technical Issues

### Q: Why is my upload failing?
**A:** Common causes:
- File too large (check plan limits)
- Unsupported format (convert to supported format)
- Poor internet connection (try again with stable connection)
- Browser issues (clear cache, try different browser)

### Q: My content is stuck in processing. What should I do?
**A:** Processing can take up to 24 hours for large files. If stuck longer:
1. Check your internet connection remained stable
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Contact support if problem persists

### Q: Which browsers work best with Ainflue?
**A:** We support:
- Chrome 90+ (recommended)
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+
- Edge 90+

Older browsers may have limited functionality.

### Q: Can I use Ainflue on mobile devices?
**A:** Yes! Our web platform is mobile-responsive. We also have dedicated iOS and Android apps with full feature parity.

## Platform Integrations

### Q: How do I connect my social media accounts?
**A:** Go to Settings → Platform Connections. Click "Connect" next to each platform and follow the OAuth authorization process. We never access passwords directly.

### Q: What permissions do you need for platform connections?
**A:** We request minimal necessary permissions:
- YouTube: Read analytics, view channel info
- Instagram: Read insights, view posts
- TikTok: Read analytics, view videos
We never post content or modify your accounts.

### Q: Can I disconnect platforms anytime?
**A:** Yes, you can disconnect platforms instantly from Settings. We'll immediately stop accessing data from disconnected platforms.

### Q: Do you support enterprise platform connections?
**A:** Yes, Enterprise plans include bulk platform connections, team account management, and advanced integration options.
